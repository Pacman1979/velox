Section 2

Website builds

One-page site — the build is free when paired with an active care plan. If the care plan is cancelled within the first 3 months, a build fee of $294 becomes payable. This covers the cost of the work done.

Full website ($997) — a one-off fee covering the scope agreed before work starts. The $997 build includes:

  • Up to 6 pages
  • Custom design in your brand colours and fonts
  • Copy editing — you supply the words, we shape them
  • Contact form wired to your inbox
  • Mobile responsive layout
  • Basic on-page SEO — titles, meta descriptions, heading structure
  • Google Business Profile connection

Timeline: Studio Velox will complete builds within a reasonable timeframe, typically 7–14 days from receiving all required content from you. Delays caused by late content from the client are not Studio Velox's responsibility.

Section 3

The care plan

The $147/month (or $1,470/year) care plan covers:

  • Hosting, domain connection and SSL certificate
  • Small changes — prices, dates, photos, hours, wording
  • Fixing anything that breaks
  • Backups and uptime monitoring
  • Business email forwarding to your normal inbox
  • Software and security updates

The care plan does not include new pages or sections, redesigns, advertising, SEO campaigns, AI tools, copywriting, photography, or third-party costs such as domain registration fees or booking platform subscriptions.

A "small change" is anything that takes well under an hour. If a request is larger than that, you'll be told the cost before any work is done — never after.

Section 8

Leaving and handover

You can cancel the care plan at any time. Monthly plans: cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period. Annual plans: cancellation takes effect at the end of the paid year — no refund for unused months.

If you leave, Studio Velox will:

  • Transfer the GitHub repository to your own account (or provide the files as a download)
  • Assist with pointing your domain to a new host
  • Provide a clean handover within 14 days of your cancellation request

If your site uses Cloudflare Email Routing through the Studio Velox account, you will need to set up your own Cloudflare account and reconfigure email routing. Studio Velox will provide clear written instructions to make this straightforward.

The short version: if you leave, you take your site with you. There are no lock-ins, no held hostage domains, and no gotchas.
