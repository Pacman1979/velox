Six reasons the locals come here instead.

The lake does the babysitting

Bring a bag of duck food and the kids are occupied for half an hour. The playground's across the road when they're done.

You'll get a park

Generous parking right out front. Not "there's usually something around the corner" — actual spaces, most of the time.

The dog gets a menu too

Shaded outdoor seating, a water bowl, and treats on the menu. He's not tied to a pole watching you eat.

Coffee worth the drive

Melbourne roaster Locale, pulled properly. Plus a loose-leaf tea list longer than most cafés' whole drinks menu.

The walls change every month

A rotating exhibition from Queensland and Gold Coast artists. Different coffee, different art, every couple of months.

Open at six. Every day.

Before work, after the walk, or on the way to the beach. We're on from 6am seven days — including Monday.

What to order the first time.

Breakfast and lunch, cooked to order by Corey and Kumi. Gluten free and vegan options across the menu.

Harissa Lamb Burger The signature

Spiced lamb mince patty, cos, cucumber, pickled red onion, harissa mayo and orange relish on a milk bun — with Moroccan hash cubes.

Lakeview Potato Hash Cakes Gluten free

Our own hash cakes with pepita seeds and almond flakes. The one people come back for and then order every time.

Eggs Benedict

Done properly. The most-mentioned dish in our reviews, and we're not going to change it.

The Beef Burger

One regular calls it the best beef burger on the Gold Coast. We'd like you to come and settle the argument.

French Toast & Waffles

Weekend territory. Eggs your way as well — poached, scrambled, fried, on whatever you like.

Mango Bang & the smoothie list Vegan options

Plus vegan protein blends, and a loose-leaf tea selection that takes a minute to read.

On the house

A free bag of duck food with every kids' breakfast.

Because the ducks are the real reason they want to come — and because half an hour of quiet is worth more than a discount.

We're not going to compete on price.

There are a hundred places on the Gold Coast to buy a coffee, and some of them are cheaper than us. None of them come with a lake, a gazebo, a playground across the road and a bag of duck food for the kids.

That's the bit you can't get on James Street at any price.
