Plain answers

Is there an annual option?

Yes, on all three. Pay for 12 months and you only pay for 10 — $470 instead of $564, $970 instead of $1,164, or $1,470 instead of $1,764. The build fee is unchanged either way. Annual plans are non-refundable, but if you cancel you keep the site.

Is there a free trial?

Yes, and it starts when the site is finished rather than when you enquire. The one-page site gives you 14 days; the five-page options give you 7 days. Nothing is charged until it ends, and you'll get a reminder first. During the trial the site runs on a Studio Velox address — it moves onto your own domain once the first payment clears.

Is the one-page site really free?

The build is, and there's no catch in the small print. I'd rather look after a site properly for years than charge a big fee once and disappear — so the free build only makes sense alongside the monthly plan. I'm also at the stage of wanting to help people get off the ground, and a $1,500 quote is what usually stops them.

What counts as a small change?

Anything I can do in well under an hour. New dates, a price update, swapping a photo, fixing a typo, adding a review. If a request is bigger than that I'll tell you before doing it, never after.

What if I need it faster than 7 days?

Ask. The honest answer is that 7 days is what I can promise everybody, and I'd rather not quietly push someone else's job back. If it's genuinely urgent there's usually a way to do it, and it'll be priced accordingly so it's fair on the people already waiting.

What if I just want to buy it outright?

That can be arranged — we agree a figure for the site, you own it, and that's the end of it. Anything you need afterwards is $80/hr. It costs more up front than the monthly, because the monthly is what pays for looking after it.

How do I ask for a change?

Message me. No ticket system and no portal to learn. Most changes are live the same day.

Who owns the site and the domain?

You do. The domain is registered in your name and the website is yours. If you ever leave, you take it with you.

Is there a minimum term on the free build?

No lock-in, but one fair condition: if you cancel the plan within the first three months, a build fee of $294 becomes payable. That covers the work already done. After three months there's nothing to pay and you keep the site either way.

How does billing work?

Card payment through Stripe. The monthly fee comes out automatically on the same date each month, and you can update your card or manage the plan yourself at any time.
